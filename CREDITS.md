# Credits — third-party assets

All third-party assets live under `assets/` and are resolved by game id at runtime
(`src/ui/ui_assets.gd`). Sources and licenses:

## Sprites — Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles (CC0)

Enemy, character, and race sprites (`assets/sprites/`) are from the
[crawl/tiles](https://github.com/crawl/tiles) export of Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup's
tileset, released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) by
the artists listed in that repository's `ARTISTS.md`. No attribution required —
given with thanks anyway.

## Icons — game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0 — attribution REQUIRED)

Card, status, relic, and map-node icons (`assets/icons/`) are from
[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), licensed
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Icons by **Lorc, Delapouite, Skoll, Felbrigg, and other game-icons.net
contributors** (per-icon authorship at game-icons.net). This credit must ship with
any distributed build.

## UI + sound effects — Kenney (CC0)

Card frame and panel art (`assets/ui/`) from Kenney's **UI Pack**; sound effects
(`assets/audio/sfx/`) from Kenney's **Casino Audio**, **Impact Sounds**, and
**Interface Sounds** packs — all [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl), CC0. No
attribution required — support him if you can.

## Music — OpenGameArt CC0 collection

Tracks in `assets/audio/music/` are from the
[CC0 Fantasy Music & Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-fantasy-music-sounds)
collection on OpenGameArt.org (all CC0):

- `title.mp3` — "Magic Strings — A Legend Will Rise"
- `map.mp3` — "Town Theme"
- `combat.mp3` — "Battle Theme A"
- `boss.mp3` — "Boss Theme"
- `rest.mp3` — "The Old Tower Inn"

## Engine-locked sources deliberately NOT used

RPG Maker RTP / official free materials are licensed solely for games made with
RPG Maker (EULA Article 2) and are excluded from this project. See
`docs/progress/2026-06-09-asset-sourcing.md`.
