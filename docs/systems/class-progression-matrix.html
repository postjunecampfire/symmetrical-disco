Class Progression Matrix

Pick your class at Act 3, then make a branching choice of two at Acts 6, 9 and 12 (1→2→4→8 capstones). Act 15 is universal Ascension: whatever capstone you reached becomes Ascended X — a flat power bump to every card in your deck plus that capstone's signature Ult. Before Act 3 you're the race-only "normal person" (Journeyman / Acolyte / Pickpocket). Naming leans on Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones; roles map to the game's mechanics (block = DEX, poison ignores block, Vulnerable/Frail, crits, summons).
